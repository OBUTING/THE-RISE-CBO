About Us

Our story, rooted in Kahawa West

From a small youth group to a four-pillar community organisation — here's how The Rise CBO grew, and why.

Founding & Evolution

How we got here

  1. 2017

    A small youth group is born

    Mary Muthoni Simiyu founds The Rise CBO in Kahawa West, bringing together local youth around mentorship and community activities.

  2. 2020

    Pandemic expansion

    As COVID-19 disrupts schooling and family life, The Rise CBO opens safe-space sessions and children's mentorship to support families through crisis.

  3. Since

    Listening to the community

    We see rising teenage pregnancies, school dropouts and limited economic opportunity for women up close — and respond with new women's empowerment, mental health and skills-training programs.

  4. Today

    Four pillars, growing reach

    Education, Women Empowerment, Mental Health & SRHR, and Sports & Talent Development now work together as one community ecosystem.

Founder Spotlight

Mary Muthoni Simiyu

Mary founded The Rise CBO in 2017 and continues to lead alongside a growing circle of grassroots community leaders, volunteers and mentors who give their time to Kahawa West's children, youth and women.

Mission

To provide a nurturing space for children, youth and women to learn, express creativity and grow their talents — while mentoring young mothers to overcome challenges and build sustainable futures.

Vision

A generation where every talent, education and mentorship creates lasting empowerment and opportunities for all in Kahawa West and beyond.

What Guides Us

Our core values

Integrity

We do right by our community, even when no one is watching.

Innovation

We find creative, local solutions to real community needs.

Collaboration

We grow faster together — with families, volunteers and partners.

Accountability

We own our results and are honest about what still needs work.

Sustainability

We build skills and systems that outlast any single program.

Our Approach

How we create impact

Safe spaces first

Every program starts with a physically and emotionally safe place to show up — the library, mentorship camps and training sessions all begin here.

Tackling root causes

We work directly against the drivers of teenage pregnancy and school dropout — through mentorship, SRHR awareness and keeping kids engaged in learning.

Economic resilience

Vocational skills and micro-savings groups give women practical, income-generating paths forward — not just one-off support.

The Rise CBO

A grassroots community organisation in Kahawa West, Nairobi — empowering women, youth and children through education, mentorship and skills since 2017.
