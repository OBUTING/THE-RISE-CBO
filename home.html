Our Story

Grown from the community, for the community

The Rise CBO was founded in 2017 in Kahawa West by Mary Muthoni Simiyu as a small youth group. When the COVID-19 pandemic hit in 2020, it expanded to offer children's mentorship and safe-space sessions — and kept growing as we saw the need for women's economic opportunity, mental health awareness and youth talent development up close.

What We Do

Four pillars, one community

Everything we do fits under four programs, each meeting a real need we see in Kahawa West every day.

Education

A community library, homework help and school-fee sponsorship for children who need it.

Women Empowerment

Vocational training in beadwork, poultry farming, crochet and financial literacy for women and young mothers.

Mental Health & SRHR

Holiday mentorship camps, mental wellness workshops and sanitary support for the community.

Sports & Talent

Art, dance, chess, football and Kho Kho — building fitness, confidence and teamwork.
