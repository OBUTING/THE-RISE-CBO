Programs

Four pillars of change

Explore what each program looks like day-to-day — and how to request support or partner with us.

Education

Community Library & Learning Support

Our Kahawa West community library gives children a safe, quiet place to learn — with real support around them, not just a room with books.

  • Homework assistance & reading clubs
  • Academic progress monitoring
  • School fees & supplies sponsorship for families in need
Women Empowerment

Skills that build income

Practical, sellable vocational skills for women and young mothers — paired with the financial literacy to make them sustainable.

  • Beadwork, crocheting & poultry farming training
  • Soap making & other micro-enterprise skills
  • Micro-savings groups & financial literacy
Mental Health & SRHR

Wellness & reproductive health awareness

Holiday mentorship camps that give young people space to talk openly about mental health and sexual & reproductive health rights — without stigma.

  • School-holiday youth mentorship camps
  • Mental wellness workshops
  • SRHR awareness & sanitary product support
Sports & Talent

Fitness, discipline & teamwork

From the football pitch to the chess table, we give youth structured ways to build confidence and community alongside their peers.

  • Art, dance & chess clubs
  • Football coaching
  • Kho Kho training for fitness & teamwork
The Rise CBO

A grassroots community organisation in Kahawa West, Nairobi — empowering women, youth and children through education, mentorship and skills since 2017.
